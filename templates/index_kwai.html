    Is it free to download Kwai videos?

    Yes, SaveLinkX is completely free. No account, subscription, or payment of any kind is required.

    Which Kwai links work?

    Video links in the format kwai.com/@username/video/1234567890 (a numeric video ID) are supported, on kwai.com, www.kwai.com, or m.kwai.com. Profile links without /video/ in them are not supported - open the individual video first and copy that link.

    Can I choose the video quality?

    No. Kwai doesn't have a dedicated extractor, so SaveLinkX pulls the single MP4 file Kwai's own player serves - there's no resolution selector or audio-only option here, unlike on TikTok or Instagram.

    Does the download remove the Kwai watermark?

    No. You get the video exactly as Kwai's server delivers it. If a watermark or logo is already burned into the video's pixels, no tool - including SaveLinkX - can remove it.

    Can I download a private Kwai video?

    No. SaveLinkX only works with public Kwai videos. Private or restricted videos can't be processed.
